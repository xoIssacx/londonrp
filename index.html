Official FiveM Server Rules

Basic Rules

  • No RDM (Random Deathmatch)
  • No VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch)
  • No FailRP – always stay in character
  • No Metagaming or Powergaming
  • No exploiting or using glitches
  • Respect all players and staff
  • Follow all staff instructions at all times
  • No racism, discrimination, or harassment
  • No combat logging
  • Use common sense and keep RP realistic
  • No cop bating or interrupting active roleplay or staff scenes whatsoever. Always be mindful of ongoing scenes and the areas around you.
  • Do not use any sort of software that provides an unfair advantage over other players.

Emergency Services Guidelines

  • Always remain in character and follow department protocols.
  • Reckless driving whilst on duty is strictly prohibited.
  • Pirating, scavenging departmental resources to gain cash is strictly prohibited.
  • No VDM or RDM whilst on duty.
  • Chopping departmental resources is strictly prohibited. This includes vehicles.
  • Respect all players and staff
  • Follow all staff instructions at all times, this includes your higher ranks.
  • No racism, discrimination, or harassment
  • No combat logging
  • Use common sense and keep RP realistic
  • Do not engage in illegal activities whilst on duty.
  • Do not use any sort of software that provides an unfair advantage over other players.
